02 / Engineering approach

Start with the behavior.
Work through the details.

I’m drawn to the point where analysis meets implementation: defining what a system should do, understanding its interfaces, and checking what it actually does.

My current senior-design work focuses on launch-vehicle systems engineering and vehicle integration, including GNC and avionics integration. I’m building toward a career in launch-vehicle development, integration, or testing.
